# Decime UY — Product Brief (Next Phase)

**Version:** 0.3  
**Date:** 2026-06-23

---

# Main product goal

The next phase is focused on turning Decime UY from a feature-rich MVP into a stable everyday communication tool.

The product is not intended to become a generic AI language platform.

The core value proposition remains:

> Understand messages, reply naturally in Uruguay Spanish, and gradually learn from real conversations.

---

# Primary use case

The primary scenario is:

```text
Receive WhatsApp message
↓
Share into Decime UY
↓
Understand meaning
↓
Generate reply
↓
Copy or share back
↓
Optionally learn 1–3 new items
```

Everything else is secondary.

---

# Product priorities

Priority order:

## P0

Reliable everyday use.

## P1

No data loss.

## P2

Stable Android experience.

## P3

Personal VM deployment.

## P4

Small beta with real users.

## P5

Analytics and feedback loops.

---

# Main screens

## Quick Reply

This is the main screen.

Must optimize for:

- speed;
- minimum taps;
- low cognitive load.

Target:

20–30 seconds from incoming message to sent reply.

---

## Compose

Advanced mode.

Used less frequently.

---

## Training

Secondary value layer.

Users should never feel forced into studying.

Learning should emerge naturally from communication.

---

## History

Reference tool.

Not the center of the product.

---

# Things deliberately postponed

Not planned for this phase:

- Twilio;
- WhatsApp bots;
- autonomous agents;
- LangGraph;
- RAG;
- vector databases;
- realtime voice;
- multi-country support;
- expression extraction;
- courses and lessons.

---

# Target audience

Primary audience:

- immigrants in Uruguay;
- A1–B1 level;
- WhatsApp-heavy communication;
- Russian and English speakers.

---

# Next milestones

---

## Milestone 1

Persistent storage.

Success criteria:

No loss of training or history after API restart.

---

## Milestone 2

Android device QA.

Success criteria:

Complete end-to-end flow on real phones.

---

## Milestone 3

VM deployment.

Success criteria:

HTTPS instance with authentication enabled.

---

## Milestone 4

5–10 beta users.

Goals:

Collect:

- errors;
- prompts that fail;
- unnatural phrases;
- UX friction.

---

## Milestone 5

Analytics.

Measure:

- DAU;
- replies generated;
- approval rate;
- reviews completed;
- retention.

---

# Success metric

The most important metric is not number of features.

It is:

> How many times per week users successfully answer real WhatsApp conversations with Decime UY.
